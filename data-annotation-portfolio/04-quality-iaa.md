# Annotation Quality & Inter-Annotator Agreement

## Bootcamp workbook exercise
- Total reviewed items: 20
- Agreements: 15
- Disagreements: 5
- Percentage agreement: **75%**

Formula: percentage agreement = (agreements / total reviewed items) × 100  
(15 / 20) × 100 = 75%.

This is a result from a bootcamp practice workbook, not a workplace performance score.

## Selected adjudication examples
| Exercise item | Adjudicated label |
|---|---|
| #3 — package arrived Tuesday | Neutral |
| #7 — sarcastic comment about a purchase | Negative |
| #11 — amazing sound, poor battery | Mixed |
| #15 — sarcastic comment about a broken charger | Negative |
| #18 — does what it says on the box | Neutral |

Disagreements can reveal unclear guidelines or edge cases. Review the evidence, document a clear ruling, update guidance where needed and recalibrate annotators.
