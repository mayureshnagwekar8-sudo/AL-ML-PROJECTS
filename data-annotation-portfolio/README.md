This folder documents practical exercises completed during an **AI/ML Data Annotation Bootcamp**. It is a learning portfolio, not evidence of paid annotation employment or production client work.

## Areas covered
- **Image & video:** CVAT annotation concepts, bounding boxes, polygons, points, masks, tags, object tracking, keyframes, interpolation, occlusion and outside-frame handling.
- **Text:** sentiment and intent classification, spam detection, named-entity recognition (NER).
- **Audio:** clean/verbatim transcription, speaker diarization, sound-event tags, inaudible and crosstalk handling.
- **Quality:** guideline adherence, calibration, review, gold checks, percentage agreement, adjudication and edge-case escalation.
- **LLM evaluation:** instruction following, factual accuracy, clarity and usefulness.

## Annotation tools studied
- [CVAT](https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat) — primary hands-on tool used in bootcamp practice.
- [Label Studio](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio) — additional platform studied; listed as familiarity, not claimed as hands-on project experience.

## Scope and integrity
Examples in this folder are synthetic demonstrations created to show annotation formats and reasoning. They are not client data, native CVAT exports, or production annotations. The IAA figure shown is from a bootcamp workbook exercise. No professional annotation employment is claimed.

## Quality principles
1. Read task instructions before labeling.
2. Label only what the data and guidelines support; do not guess.
3. Apply labels and entity spans consistently.
4. Escalate ambiguous or missing-label cases.
5. Review accuracy, consistency and completeness.
6. Treat AI-generated prelabels as suggestions requiring human review.
