# Audio Annotation Practice

Bootcamp practice included transcription, speaker diarization, sound-event annotation and vocal emotion labels.

## Concepts
- **Verbatim transcription:** preserves fillers, repetitions and stutters when required by the style guide.
- **Clean transcription:** removes unnecessary disfluencies while preserving the speaker's meaning.
- **Diarization:** identifies who speaks when; speaker IDs should remain consistent.
- **Sound-event tags:** mark relevant non-speech events such as a dog bark, alarm or keyboard typing.
- **Inaudible:** use only when speech cannot reliably be recovered.
- **Crosstalk:** use when overlapping speech prevents reliable transcription.

Always follow the project-specific audio style guide. The JSON sample in examples/ is synthetic and is not a transcript from an actual recording.
